## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment**: The product surface (modal, rail, answer templates) reads as considered and category-fluent — it would not immediately register as generated. The host site would. `WELCOME TO MERIDIAN UNIVERSITY` tracked-uppercase eyebrow, six identical icon+heading+text college cards, and a 150+/18:1/92%/$75M metric strip are three of the most saturated generated-landing-page moves, sitting on the warm near-white body (`--bg: #F7F5F0`) that is itself the 2026 AI default. PRODUCT.md names that background as anti-reference #1.

**Deterministic scan** (`detect.mjs`, 10 markup/JSX files): 5 findings.
- `overused-font` — Geist, `index.html:10` and `computer-science.html:10`
- `single-font` — same lines: **false positive**, the page loads Geist + Source Serif 4 + JetBrains Mono and uses all three
- `layout-transition` — `tweaks-panel.jsx:104` (`transition: width`)

The design hook separately flags `styles.css:36` (Geist) and `styles.css:445` / `:3573` (`transition: width`). `:445` is the rail collapse animation and looks deliberate.

**Visual overlays**: injection succeeded; the in-page detector reported 2 anti-patterns (`overused font: geist 95% of text`, `layout property animation: transition: width`) plus one `low contrast text` overlay. Overlays rendered in the automation tab, not a presented human tab.

**Where they disagree**: the detector missed everything structural — the clipped card rows, the mobile overflow, and the contrast failures I measured directly (it flagged one contrast instance; the real count is 11 elements).

## Overall Impression

The interaction model is the strong part and it is what the pitch is selling: scope tabs, streaming cited answers, a genuinely useful Degree Planner, follow-ups, role switching. What undercuts it is craft on the paths a presenter walks. Two card rows clip mid-card with no scroll affordance, so the third faculty member and the third matched program look truncated rather than scrollable. The faculty row also swallows vertical scroll, which stalls the page mid-demo. At 390px the role selector is cut off under the close button. And the trust text — `AI can make mistakes. Double check responses.` — fails contrast at 2.45:1, which is exactly the sentence a procurement-minded viewer looks for.

Biggest opportunity: the identity move you already chose. Committing to navy as a real surface, off the cream default, would fix the AI-default read and give the demo a distinct look in one pass.

## What's Working

- **Answer composition.** The CS answer is genuinely well-structured: lede with inline citations, two clear actions, then overview → courses → faculty → outcomes → campus → related programs. Section rhythm holds and nothing feels padded.
- **The Degree Planner earns its place.** Three questions, real progress indicator, `Best match` / `High demand` / `Hands-on lab` distinctions on results, and both `Explore` and `Cost & aid` exits per card. It hands off into a real answer instead of dead-ending.
- **Keyboard behavior.** ⌘K opens, Esc closes and returns focus to the Search trigger with a visible ring. That is the part demo audiences test unprompted.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Card rows clip mid-card with no scroll affordance**
- **Why it matters**: The faculty row cuts Dr. Priya Nair's card and her `Office hours` button; the Degree Planner match row cuts the Cybersecurity card and its `Cost & aid` button. Nothing indicates the row scrolls, so it reads as a rendering bug, not a carousel. The faculty row also captures the wheel, so scrolling over it stops the page from moving.
- **Fix**: Give the rows an explicit affordance (edge fade + arrow controls, or `scroll-snap` with visible partial card and a `Show all` link), and set `overscroll-behavior-x: contain` so vertical wheel events pass through. At the modal's content width, three cards do not fit; either size to two-and-a-peek deliberately or wrap to a grid.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt`

**[P1] Muted text fails contrast in 11 places, including the AI disclaimer**
- **Why it matters**: `--text-faint: #A39E92` at 11–13px measures 2.45–2.67:1 against white. It carries the inactive scope tabs (`Programs`, `Faculty`, `Campus`), all six faculty rating counts, the `you can also ask (3)` header, and `AI can make mistakes. Double check responses.` The disclaimer is the trust sentence; failing it is the worst place to fail.
- **Fix**: Retire `--text-faint` for anything that carries meaning. Move inactive tabs and the disclaimer to `--text-muted` or darker (≥4.5:1), and keep the faint tone only for non-informational marks. Inactive tab labels also need a hover/focus state that clears contrast.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable audit`

**[P1] Role selector and scope tabs overflow at 390px**
- **Why it matters**: `I'm a prospective student` is truncated under the close button in the mobile modal header, and the scope row loses `Admissions` off the right edge with no scroll cue. Prospective students are a phone-first audience and the pitch will get resized.
- **Fix**: Collapse the role selector to a compact control (icon + short label, or move it into the header menu) below ~480px, and make the scope row a scrollable strip with an edge fade.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt`

**[P2] Suggested questions fill the composer instead of running**
- **Why it matters**: Clicking `Tell me about the Computer Science major` types it into the input and stops. In a demo that reads as a dead click; the presenter has to hit the arrow. Every equivalent product (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Claude) submits on click.
- **Fix**: Submit on click. Keep fill-only behavior on a modifier or a small `edit` affordance if it was intentional.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P2] The rail defaults to nine unlabeled icons**
- **Why it matters**: A first-time prospective student cannot tell Saved from Collections from Degree Planner from Virtual Advisor without hovering each one. Tooltips are hover-only, so touch users get nothing. The agents are a selling point and they are invisible.
- **Fix**: Default the rail expanded in the modal (there is horizontal room at the 1200px panel), or show labels under icons for the agent group. Keep collapse as a user choice, not the initial state.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

**[P2] Host page is built from generated-landing-page parts**
- **Why it matters**: PRODUCT.md lists the tracked-uppercase eyebrow, identical card grids, the hero-metric strip, and the warm near-white body as anti-references. All four are on the home page. Whatever the modal earns, the frame around it is the first thing on screen.
- **Fix**: Execute the committed-color strategy — navy carrying 30–60% of the surface, cream retired as the body — then rebuild the college section on a non-uniform composition and fold the four metrics into prose or a single deliberate figure. Drop the eyebrow.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable colorize`

**[P3] No empty, no-results, or error state exists**
- **Why it matters**: A query outside the template set is the most likely accident in a live demo, and there is nothing designed for it. `Error Recovery` scores 1 for this reason.
- **Fix**: One graceful fallback: restate the query, offer the four canned questions and the scope tabs, no apology copy.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

## Minor Observations

- Degree Planner never echoes prior answers — step 2 shows a check on `Interest` but not `Engineering & Computing`.
- Back on planner steps is full-width, wider and more prominent than the three option cards it returns from.
- `you can also ask (3)` is lowercase monospace with a count; every other label on the surface is sentence case.
- Numbered mono labels (`1 · WHAT ARE YOU INTERESTED IN?`, `STEP 1 OF 3`) stack two scaffolding devices on a three-step flow that already has a progress row.
- Campus cards are inconsistent: Hale has a description line, Innovation Studio's rating sits a row higher, so the `Tour`/`Map` buttons do not align.
- Short states (landing, planner steps) leave 250–300px of empty panel below content; landing centers vertically, planner top-aligns.
- Feedback row (copy / share / retry / thumbs) is icon-only and unlabeled.
- No source list anywhere — citations open a popover one at a time, so `cite or don't claim` has no summary view.

## Questions to Consider

- The Degree Planner is the most differentiated thing in the demo and it is behind an unlabeled icon. What if it were the landing state for prospective students instead of a rail item?
- What would this look like if the answer showed its sources as a set rather than one popover at a time?
- Three of the six scope tabs are nearly invisible. Are six scopes real, or is this three plus a menu?
